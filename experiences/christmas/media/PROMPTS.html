Station 1 · The Promise

Prompt sheet for Video Express AI. Make 4 shots (plus optional talking clips), save them in this media folder with the exact file names shown, then open media.html.

How to make each shot in Video Express

  1. Open Text to Video. Paste the prompt below (the style and character text is already included, so every shot matches).
  2. Set aspect ratio to 16:9 and pick a realistic or cinematic style.
  3. Click Generate reference image. If the image isn't right, regenerate it before making the video. This is the step where you control how the people look.
  4. Create the video, then download it and rename it to the file name shown (for example s1-01-night.mp4).
  5. If a video comes out badly, you can save just the reference image instead (for example s1-01-night.jpg). The player slowly pans across stills, so they still feel alive.

Clips around 10 seconds are fine. The player loops the background shots for as long as the narration needs. Turn off any music or voice in Video Express. The narrator and character voices come from the Christmas Experience itself.

The four shots

Shot 1 · Night over the hills of Judea

s1-01-night.mp4
Used for: the opening and the three star taps
PromptCinematic photorealistic wide establishing shot at night of the rolling hills of ancient Judea, around 700 BC. A vast, clear sky full of stars fills the top half of the frame, with no moon and nothing in the upper 45 percent of the image except stars and a faint Milky Way. On a distant hill at the right sits a small village of flat-roofed stone houses with a few warm oil-lamp windows. In the left foreground a lone robed figure with a wooden staff stands on a hilltop, small in the frame, looking up at the sky. Cool moonlit blues and deep indigo, gentle mist in the valleys. Very slow, steady push-in. Natural film lighting, 35mm film look, muted earthy palette. No text, no logos, no modern objects, no people close to camera.

Keep the upper sky empty. The player draws the three glowing prophecy stars there for visitors to tap.

Shot 2 · Isaiah writing by lamplight

s1-02-isaiah.mp4
Used for: Isaiah 7:14, 9:6 and 9:2
PromptCinematic photorealistic medium close-up, side profile facing right, of Isaiah, a Hebrew prophet in his late 60s: weathered olive-brown skin, deep-set dark eyes, a long flowing white-grey beard reaching his chest, a cream linen head covering with a thin dark-red band, a deep wine-red wool robe under a dark plum mantle. He sits at a rough wooden table in a stone room at night, writing slowly on an unrolled parchment scroll with a reed pen. A small clay oil lamp on the table lights his face with warm golden candlelight, chiaroscuro like a Georges de La Tour painting. Behind him an arched stone window shows the night sky with one bright star. The lamp flame flickers; he pauses and lifts his eyes toward the star. Ancient Judea around 700 BC, 35mm film look, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no modern objects.

Shot 3 · Micah on the hill above Bethlehem

s1-03-micah.mp4
Used for: Micah 5:2
PromptCinematic photorealistic medium close-up, side profile facing left, of Micah, a Hebrew prophet in his late 30s from the countryside: sun-darkened olive-brown skin, a short thick dark-brown beard, an earthy brown head cloth with a slate-blue band, an olive-green tunic and a rough brown wool cloak. He stands on a moonlit hilltop at night, gazing toward the tiny village of Bethlehem on a far hill with a few warm lamp-lit windows. A single bright star shines in the sky above the village. A light wind moves his cloak and head cloth; cool blue moonlight rims his face. Ancient Judea around 700 BC, 35mm film look, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no modern objects.

Shot 4 · Dawn breaks over the hills

s1-04-dawn.mp4
Used for: the ending, "have seen a great light"
PromptCinematic photorealistic wide shot of the rolling hills of ancient Judea at sunrise, the same landscape as the night shot: a small village of flat-roofed stone houses on a distant hill at the right and a lone robed figure with a staff on a foreground hill at the left. Golden sunlight spills over the horizon, the sky glows from deep blue to rose and gold, the last stars fade, and warm light washes across the hills and mist. Slow, majestic push-in, hopeful mood. 35mm film look, natural light. No text, no logos, no modern objects.

Optional · The prophets speaking (lip-synced)

If your plan includes Talking Photos (All-Access), you can make Isaiah and Micah actually speak their lines in the same voices as the rest of the experience.

  1. Take a still of the prophet: the reference image from Shot 2 or 3, ideally one where he faces the camera at a slight angle. Lip-sync works best when the mouth is clearly visible.
  2. In Talking Photos, upload the still and the matching voice file from media/voices/ (upload the audio, don't type the text, so the voice matches).
  3. Save the result with the file name below. The player then plays it during that verse, and the narrator reads the reference afterward.

Keeping the people consistent

  • Paste the prompts exactly. Each one repeats the same description of the prophet and the same style words.
  • If your plan has Character Consistency, save Isaiah from your best Shot 2 reference image as a character, and use him again for any later Isaiah shots.
  • Pick reference images where the clothing colors match the description (Isaiah: wine-red robe and cream head covering; Micah: brown head cloth with a blue band).
